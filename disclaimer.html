01 Scope

1. General Information

The information available on JobUtsav.com is provided strictly for general informational and recruitment facilitation purposes.

JobUtsav operates as an event facilitator and connection platform bringing together hiring corporate organizations, colleges, sponsors, and job seekers. JobUtsav does not act as an direct employer for participating companies unless explicitly documented under a formal written agreement.

02 Important Notice

2. No Employment Guarantee

JobUtsav does not guarantee employment offers, interview calls, selection outcomes, job offers, salary benchmarks, or placement success.

Hiring Discretion Notice

All shortlisting, interview evaluations, background checks, and final employment offers remain at the sole and independent discretion of participating recruiters and employer representatives.

Candidates are strongly encouraged to prepare thoroughly, present authentic credentials, and interview proactively across multiple company booths.

03 Verification

3. Accuracy of Information

While JobUtsav takes rigorous measures to verify participating companies and publish authentic job fair schedules, we make no explicit or implied warranties regarding the completeness, accuracy, reliability, or timeliness of all third-party details published on the website.

Users are advised to independently evaluate job specifications, compensation structures, employer credibility, and contract conditions before accepting offers or taking employment decisions.

04 External Entities

4. Third-Party Information & Listings

Company profiles, job requirements, salary bands, and institutional details are provided directly by external corporate employers, college placement cells, and sponsors.

JobUtsav does not endorse or validate all internal hiring processes of third-party organizations and shall not be held responsible for inaccuracies, cancellations, or modifications made by independent recruiters.

05 Outbound Links

5. External Links Disclaimer

JobUtsav.com may contain hyperlinks directing visitors to third-party web portals, employer application systems, or partner platforms.

These external links are provided solely for user convenience. JobUtsav maintains no editorial or administrative control over external websites, and does not accept liability for their content, security standards, cookie practices, or commercial terms.

06 Operations

6. Event and Service Availability

Job fair calendar dates, venue expo halls, company attendance lists, and booth allocations may be rescheduled or updated due to logistical requirements, venue availability, government directives, or unforeseen force majeure events.

Advance Event Communication

JobUtsav sends registered candidates direct SMS, WhatsApp, and email alerts regarding any venue or schedule changes well in advance of the hiring dates.
