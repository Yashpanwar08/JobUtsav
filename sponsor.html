Sponsorship Overview

About JobUtsav

JobUtsav is a transformative, multi-city hiring initiative designed to bridge the gap between job seekers and employers by creating a high-impact, on-ground recruitment ecosystem that connects talent with opportunity at unprecedented scale.

What JobUtsav Delivers

This landmark initiative brings together multiple stakeholders including educational institutions, training centers, and corporate employers - all working towards a shared vision of accessible employment opportunities at scale.

Event Details & Vision

  • Event: JobUtsav - India's Hiring Festival
  • Location: Greater Noida
  • Dates: September 13-14
  • Duration: 2 Days
  • Entry: Free for all candidates
  • Expected Turnout: 10,000+ Candidates

Our Mission

  • Enable mass hiring in a structured environment
  • Provide direct employment access to job seekers nationwide
  • Help companies hire efficiently at scale
  • Create a recurring hiring platform across cities
  • Create social impact by generating employment & earning opportunities
10,000+ Candidates Freshers, experienced professionals, ITI graduates, and diploma holders seeking career opportunities.
100+ Hiring Companies Diverse industries including IT, manufacturing, healthcare, BPO, and retail sectors.

Job Seekers

Corporate Partners

Established corporates with hiring needs; fast-growing startups seeking talent; companies across multiple industries.

Educational Institutions

Colleges and universities; polytechnics and training institutes; skilling and certification partners.
