About JobUtsav

JobUtsav is a transformative, multi-city hiring initiative designed to bridge the gap between job seekers and employers by creating a high-impact, on-ground recruitment ecosystem that connects talent with opportunity at unprecedented scale.

This landmark initiative brings together multiple stakeholders including educational institutions, training centers, and corporate employers — all working towards a shared vision of accessible employment opportunities at scale.

Our Mission
Enable mass hiring in a structured environment
Provide direct employment access to job seekers nationwide
Help companies hire efficiently at scale
Create a recurring hiring platform across cities
Create social impact by generating employment & earning opportunities
