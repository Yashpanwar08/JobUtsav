Users are solely responsible for the authenticity of the resumes, credentials, job postings, and communications transmitted via JobUtsav.

Job seekers attending JobUtsav festivals benefit from free walk-in interview access and agree to uphold the following standards:

Candidates Agree to

  • Conduct themselves professionally and respectfully during recruiter interviews and venue visits

Recruiters & Employers Must

  • Never demand any registration fees, interview fees, or training charges from job seekers
  • Handle candidate resumes and contact data with strict confidentiality in accordance with data privacy laws
  • Adhere to all statutory labour laws, non-discrimination standards, and equal opportunity principles
