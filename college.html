Helping Students Build Successful Careers

Academic excellence alone is often not enough to secure employment. JobUtsav bridges education and employment through organised hiring events and career-focused initiatives.

Our goal is to help institutions improve student employability by connecting them with employers from various sectors.

  • Career Guidance

    Exposure to real hiring practices and career opportunities.

  • Industry Interaction

    Connect directly with recruiters and professionals from multiple sectors.

  • Internship Access

    Explore internship programs that build practical experience.

  • Placement Support

    Increase student visibility among participating employers.

Building Industry–Academia Connections

Strong collaboration between educational institutions and employers plays a crucial role in improving placement outcomes and student readiness.

JobUtsav brings recruiters and academic institutions onto one platform where both can benefit from long-term hiring partnerships.

Why Career Exposure Matters

  • Improves communication
  • Clarifies employer expectations
