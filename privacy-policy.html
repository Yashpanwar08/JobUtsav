01 Overview

1. Introduction

Welcome to JobUtsav.com ("JobUtsav", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This policy applies when you visit our website, register for job fairs, submit job applications, participate in recruitment activities, contact us, or use any related career services offered through JobUtsav.

By accessing or using JobUtsav.com, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

02 Data Types

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from users, through online registration forms, during career event check-ins, through recruiter participation submissions, and automatically via secure web technologies.

Candidate & Personal Information

  • Full name, date of birth, and gender (where voluntarily provided)
  • Email address, primary mobile number, and residential city/state
  • Educational qualifications, graduation year, and academic records
  • Technical skills, domain proficiencies, and work experience history
  • Resume / CV files, portfolio links, and profile photographs

Employer & Partner Organization Information

  • Company, college, or sponsoring institution name and official URL
  • Contact representative name, official designation, and business email
  • Official contact phone number and registered corporate address
  • Hiring criteria, vacant job positions, and event booth participation details

Automatically Collected Technical Information

  • IP address, network provider details, and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, operating system version, and device characteristics
  • Pages visited, duration of visit, and referral URLs
  • Essential cookies and session tokens used for form and state management
03 Purpose

3. How We Use Your Information

We collect and process your information strictly for legitimate operational purposes to provide, coordinate, improve, and secure JobUtsav hiring events.

Recruitment & Event Operations

  • Issuing candidate registration badges, gate passes, and entry QR codes
  • Matching candidate qualifications with verified participating employers
  • Sharing candidate CVs with authorized recruiters for shortlisted roles
  • Facilitating on-spot interview scheduling and walk-in hiring processes

Direct Notifications & Event Updates

  • Sending event venue details, dates, and interview slot reminders
  • Notifying candidates when top employers announce relevant vacancies
  • Responding to inquiries, helpdesk tickets, and candidate support requests
  • Important service advisories regarding safety, schedules, and check-in times

Platform Security & Compliance

  • Monitoring website security, detecting spam, and preventing fraudulent entries
  • Analyzing aggregate event attendance metrics to optimize future fair venues
  • Complying with applicable statutory guidelines and legal obligations
04 Data Sharing

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties. We will never sell, rent, or trade candidate phone numbers, emails, or resumes to third-party telemarketers or advertisers.

Candidate-First Trust Guarantee

Your resume and contact information are only transmitted to authenticated employers participating in our hiring festivals to consider you for active employment opportunities.

Information may be disclosed solely under the following transparent conditions:

Authorized Disclosures Include

  • Verified Employers & Recruiters: Companies interviewing candidates at JobUtsav hiring booths.
  • Partner Academic Institutions: Colleges collaborating on student placements and career fairs.
  • Vetted Infrastructure Providers: Secure cloud servers, SMS/WhatsApp notification gateways, and badge printing services under confidentiality contracts.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Government departments or law enforcement agencies when strictly mandated by law or court order.
05 Lifecycle

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the recruitment and fair coordination purposes outlined in this policy, maintain accurate business records, and satisfy legal compliance duties.

When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it. Candidates may also submit an expedited data erasure request at any time by contacting our privacy officer.

06 Security

6. Data Security

JobUtsav employs comprehensive administrative, physical, and technical controls to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, manipulation, and destruction.

Security Protocols

All data transmission is encrypted via Secure Sockets Layer (SSL/TLS). Our backend infrastructure employs strict role-based access management, continuous vulnerability monitoring, and firewall defenses.

Please note that while we implement industry-standard defenses, no transmission over the internet or electronic storage method can guarantee 100% absolute invulnerability.

07 Candidate Rights

7. Your Rights & Choices

In alignment with Indian data protection laws and international best practices, you possess defined rights regarding your personal records:

Right to Access

Request an itemized copy of all personal records we maintain about you.

Right to Rectify

Correct inaccurate details or update outdated resume and contact entries.

Right to Erase

Request complete deletion of your registration profile from our active systems.

Right to Opt-Out

Unsubscribe from marketing announcements and non-essential notifications at any time.

08 External Links

8. Third-Party Websites

JobUtsav.com may contain links to third-party employer career sites, educational partners, or payment gateways. We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices, terms, or data handling protocols of those external domains.

We recommend reviewing the specific privacy statements of every third-party site you visit.

09 Revisions

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our fair operations, service enhancements, or legislative mandates.

Any updates will be promptly published on this page with an updated "Effective Date". Continued use of JobUtsav services signifies your acknowledgment of the updated terms.
