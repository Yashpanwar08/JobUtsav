01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, mobile device, or browser when you visit a website. They help websites remember user preferences, improve functionality, and understand how visitors interact with pages.

02

How We Use Cookies

JobUtsav uses cookies and similar technologies to improve the website experience and support recruitment-related services.

We use cookies to

  • Improve website performance and loading behavior
  • Enhance user experience across pages
  • Analyze website traffic and engagement
  • Remember user preferences and settings
  • Maintain website security
  • Support registration, form submission, and login processes where applicable
  • Optimize job fair and event registration workflows
03

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential cookies

  • Required for core website functionality, security, session handling, and reliable form operations.

Functional cookies

  • Help remember preferences such as selected city, form choices, or user settings where available.

Performance cookies

  • Help us understand website speed, usage patterns, and areas where performance can be improved.

Analytics cookies

  • Provide statistical information about visitor behavior, traffic sources, popular pages, and engagement.

Marketing cookies

  • May be used to deliver relevant promotional content, event updates, or advertisements where permitted by law and consent preferences.
04

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services used for analytics, embedded content, advertising, communication tools, or website infrastructure. These third parties may process data according to their own policies.

05

Managing Cookies

Users can accept, reject, delete, or disable cookies through browser settings. Most browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences from their privacy or security settings.

If cookies are disabled, some website features, forms, registrations, or user experience improvements may not function properly.

06

Updates to This Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in technology, law, or business practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

07
