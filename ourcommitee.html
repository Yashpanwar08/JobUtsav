Core Purpose

About InternBoot & Shikshit

A collaborative alliance committed to democratizing access to high-impact jobs, skills, and scholarships.

Our Mission

To forge an inclusive education and recruitment ecosystem where every student, regardless of financial background or geographic location, receives industry-aligned training that translates directly into professional employment.

  • Empower students through Indian National Science Congress (INSC) programs.
  • Eliminate recruitment middlemen with transparent, walk-in mega job fairs.
  • Foster technical competence with live, hands-on enterprise case studies.

Our Vision

An India where every graduate achieves gainful employment through equitable education, structured virtual internships, and verifiable merit credentials.

  • Empower 1 Million Students nationwide with vocational programs by 2030.
  • Scale the NS-MAS National Merit Scholarship to 100% free registrations.
  • Drive national economic growth through skilled, day-one-ready young talent.
