About Interboot & Shikshit

We are dedicated organizations committed to creating transformative change through education, skill development, and community empowerment across India.

Our Mission

To create a dynamic, inclusive educational ecosystem where every student - regardless of financial background - has access to high-quality, industry-aligned learning that converts academic potential into professional performance. We empower students with INSC (Indian National Science Congress) and bridge the gap between classroom theory and corporate practice.

Our mission extends to elevating the quality of education through best practices, innovative methodologies, and resource sharing among schools, while ensuring holistic student development that complements academic achievement with social, emotional, and physical growth.

Our Vision

To build a world where every individual has access to equitable education and the opportunity to realize their full potential. We envision a society where knowledge is freely available, talent is nurtured, and every student is prepared to thrive in a globalized world through comprehensive educational programs, vocational training, and mentorship.

By 2030, we aim to reach 1 million students across India with transformative programs that break the cycle of poverty and create sustainable opportunities for millions.

Our Comprehensive Programs

From scholarships to technical training, we offer diverse opportunities to support students at every stage of their educational journey.

NS-MAS Scholarship

National Scholarship - Merit and Assistance Scheme designed to support deserving students with financial assistance for academic excellence. Free registration, merit-based selection, and direct financial support covering tuition, books, and academic needs.

Virtual Internships

Structured 45-day to 6-month programs in 15+ industry domains with real project exposure. 100% virtual, zero cost to learners, with industry-aligned curricula co-designed with 650+ corporate partners.

Mentorship & Training

15-day live mentor-led training focused on practical industry tools and real-world scenarios. Expert mentorship from industry professionals and comprehensive skill development programs.

Verified Certification

Globally recognized, QR-verified completion certificates accepted by leading companies worldwide. Every certificate is verifiable online with industry-standard assessments.

Technical Training

Comprehensive courses in Web Development, Data Science, AI/ML, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and more. UGC-approved diplomas and industry certifications for career advancement.

Olympiads & Competitions

National and international Olympiads in Mathematics, Science, Computer Science, English, and General Knowledge. Test your skills and showcase your abilities with challenging problems.

Industry Partnerships

Strong collaborations with leading companies, government bodies, and educational institutions. Direct connections to 650+ corporate partners for placement support and PPO interviews.

Community Development

Holistic development programs including life skills, leadership training, environmental awareness, mental health support, and social inclusion initiatives for well-rounded growth.

Our Journey of Impact

From humble beginnings to transforming thousands of lives through education and empowerment.

2010

Foundation of Shikshit

Shikshit established with a vision to provide quality education and scholarship opportunities to deserving students across India. Our journey began with a commitment to breaking educational barriers.

2018

Employment Express Founded

Employment Express Verband LLP founded with a vision to create an unemployment-free India. Started with a small team passionate about bridging the gap between education and employment.

2019

First 1000 Students Trained

Reached our first milestone of training 1,000 students across various skill development programs. Established partnerships with leading companies for placement opportunities.

2020

Industry Recognition & InternBoot Launch

Received recognition for excellence in skill development and placement services. Launched InternBoot platform to connect students with quality internship opportunities nationwide.

2022

NS-MAS Scholarship Program & CSR Initiatives

Launched National Scholarship - Merit and Assistance Scheme. NIIT Foundation skill enhancement programs and Manipur Care Raising technical diplomas. Operational backbone established for national scale.

2023

Global Expansion & Specialized Programs

Cybersecurity for Women program with DSCI & Microsoft. International partnerships in Sri Lanka. Rural education initiatives and academic linkages with IIMT University.

2024

Digital Transformation & Scale

AI-powered platform with predictive hiring analytics. Crossed 10,000+ students trained and 500+ corporate partnerships. Achieved 95% placement success rate with multi-state deployment.

2025

Going Global & Future Vision

Expanding international partnerships and offering global internship opportunities. Continuous innovation in skill development programs to meet evolving industry demands and reach 1 million students by 2030.
