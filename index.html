About

JobUtsav

JobUtsav is a career-focused platform designed to connect job seekers, employers, educational institutions, and industry professionals through large-scale recruitment events across India. Our mission is to create meaningful employment opportunities by bringing together talented candidates and hiring organizations under one platform.
