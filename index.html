About JobUtsav

JobUtsav is a career-focused platform designed to connect job seekers, employers, educational institutions, and industry professionals through large-scale recruitment events across India. Our mission is to create meaningful employment opportunities by bringing together talented candidates and hiring organizations under one platform.

The initiative aims to simplify recruitment, improve candidate accessibility, and support workforce development by enabling direct interaction between recruiters and job seekers. Through job fairs, networking opportunities, and hiring events, JobUtsav helps participants explore career opportunities across multiple industries.

Whether you are a student preparing for your first job, a professional seeking career advancement, or an employer looking for skilled talent, JobUtsav provides a platform designed to support long-term professional growth and meaningful industry connections.

Why Attend JobUtsav?

Recruitment events offer a unique opportunity for candidates and employers to interact directly. Unlike traditional online applications, job fairs enable real-time conversations, faster screening processes, and valuable networking opportunities.

For Candidates

Meet recruiters directly, understand industry expectations, explore multiple job opportunities, and gain valuable interview experience.

For Employers

Connect with a large pool of motivated candidates, strengthen employer branding, and accelerate recruitment efforts through direct engagement.

For Educational Institutions

Support student employability, improve placement outcomes, and strengthen industry-academia relationships through recruitment collaborations.
