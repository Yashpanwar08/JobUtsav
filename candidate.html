Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to the event? +

Bring 5-10 hard copies of your updated resume, valid government photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN/Voter ID), educational marksheets/portfolio, and be prepared for walk-in interviews with multiple recruiters.

Is there an entry fee? +

No. Candidate registration and entry are 100% free. JobUtsav never charges candidates any hidden fees or placement commissions.

Do I need to register in advance? +

Yes, advance registration is strongly recommended. Pre-registered candidates receive priority QR fast-track entry, avoid on-spot registration queues, and have their resumes shared in advance with participating recruiters.

Can I apply for multiple companies? +

Yes, absolutely! You can meet, interact, and interview with multiple employers across IT, Finance, Marketing, Sales, and Operations under one roof on the same day.

Mastering Your Interview Day

Preparation is the bridge between a walk-in interview and a confirmed offer letter. JobUtsav hosts corporate leaders across Information Technology, BFSI, Healthcare, Retail, Core Engineering, and E-commerce.

Recruiter Pro-Tip

Employers value confidence, clarity of thought, and genuine enthusiasm to learn just as much as domain technical scores.

Update & Tailor Your Resume

Highlight your latest projects, skills, and quantifiable achievements prominently at the top of your resume.

Research Participating Companies

Review the list of attending companies in advance to identify the specific openings that best match your aspirations.

Dress for Impact (Business Formals)

Professional business attire leaves a lasting positive impression and communicates focus and preparedness.

Craft Your 60-Second Elevator Pitch

Practice a crisp self-introduction summarizing your background, core strengths, and what value you offer to the team.

Carry 5-10 Hard Copies of Your Resume

Keep multiple printed copies in a neat document folder so you can sit for multiple company interviews without delay.

Practice Core Technical & HR Questions

Rehearse fundamentals, scenario-based problem solving, and common interview questions for confident delivery.
