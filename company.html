FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions For Employers

Everything you need to know about setting up a recruitment booth and closing offers.

Who can register as an employer?

Organizations of all sizes can participate, including high-growth startups, SMEs, educational institutions, multinational corporations, and large enterprises seeking scale hiring across technical, operations, and business functions.

Can companies recruit freshers?

Yes. Many participating employers attend specifically to recruit graduating students, apprentice trainees, and energetic recent graduates across engineering, management, humanities, and diploma programs.

How are candidates informed?

Registered candidates receive detailed notifications regarding attending company profiles, open role specifications, venue schedules, and shortlisting criteria through our portal and SMS/WhatsApp prior to event day.

Can employers participate in multiple events?

Yes. Companies can register for single city summits or secure multi-city corporate hiring packages across Greater Noida, Mumbai, Bangalore, and Hyderabad for consistent annual talent acquisition.

How can recruiters contact JobUtsav?

Recruiters can use our direct WhatsApp support desk, submit the employer registration form above, or contact our corporate partnerships desk for custom booth requirements and VIP cabins.
